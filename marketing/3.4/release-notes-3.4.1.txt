App Store Connect "What's New" — version 3.4.1

AI DECKS THAT PLAY BY THE RULES 🧠
Every deck the AI builds or completes is now checked against your format: at least 60 cards, no more than 4 copies of any card (reprints included), only your inks, and only sets legal in your format. A banner shows the result at a glance (Pro).

DUAL-INK CARDS
Dual-ink cards now show up in AI decks whenever your deck plays both of their inks.

TELL US HOW IT DID 👍👎
Rate any AI deck, completion, swap list, or strategy guide. Not quite right? Tell us what missed and try again. The AI uses your feedback on the next attempt (Pro).

SAVE AND COPY SHARE CARDS
Share cards can now go straight to Photos, or be copied to paste into Discord, right next to the usual share button.

ALSO IN THIS RELEASE
• Core Constructed updated for the current rotation (Fabled through Attack of the Vine!)
• Older printings of cards reprinted in a legal set are no longer marked illegal in Core decks
• Healthier AI decks: more inkable cards and a better cost curve
• Improve Deck no longer shows an unused "Add to Deck" button
