App Store Connect "What's New" — version 3.4.0

Your collection has a value. Now it has a story.

WHAT IS IT WORTH? 📈
Collection Value charts what your cards have been worth, day by day, with this week's change on top. Built from real daily market prices — not an estimate you have to take on faith (Pro).

WHAT MOVED THIS WEEK
Biggest Movers names the cards that gained and lost the most, counted across every copy you own. Highs & Lows flags anything sitting at the top or bottom of its recent range — the closest thing to a nudge to look again at that one card (Pro).

WHAT YOU PAID, AND WHAT YOU MADE
Add what you paid for a card and Cost vs Value does the rest: your gain or loss per card, per set, and overall. Cards without a recorded price stay out of the maths, so the number you see is real (Pro).

TRADE WITH YOUR EYES OPEN 🤝
Build both sides of a trade and see what each is actually worth before you shake on it. Scan cards straight in with the camera, foils included. Confirm the trade for a little send-off, then share the result (Pro).

FOIL PREMIUM
See what your foils are really worth next to the rest of your collection (Pro).
