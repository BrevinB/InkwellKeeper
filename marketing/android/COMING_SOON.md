# Android: coming soon + beta recruiting

**Goal:** find 15–20 Android beta testers, a buffer above the 12 that Google requires. Testers must stay opted in for **14 days in a row** before the app can launch on Google Play.

Images: `marketing/drafts/images/android-coming-soon/`, rendered by `marketing/android/src/make_coming_soon.py`.

## Links to fill in before posting

- `[GROUP]`: the Google Group join link, e.g. https://groups.google.com/g/inkwellkeeper-testers
- `[OPTIN]`: the Play Console closed-testing "Join on the web" link
- **Link-in-bio:** point it at a page with both links and the three steps below. A `/android` page on the site would be ideal. If the bio already holds the App Store link, use a short list page (Linktree-style) with "iPhone" and "Android beta" buttons.

**How to join (paste wherever people ask):**
1. Join the tester group: [GROUP]. Use the Google account that's on your phone.
2. Open [OPTIN] and tap **Become a tester**.
3. Install Ink Well Keeper from the Play Store link on that page.
4. Keep it installed for at least 2 weeks. That's what counts toward launch.

## Claim check (keep these true)

- **True:** free, with the same features as iOS: scan, collection value, price history, set completion, wishlist, decks, binders and boxes, trade calculator, lore counter. AI deck builder and rules assistant are Pro.
- **True:** import from the official Disney Lorcana app, Dreamborn and Collectr CSV. This is how iPhone users bring a collection over.
- **Never claim cross-platform sync.** iCloud doesn't sync to Android. If asked, say "export from iPhone, import on Android."
- **No launch date.** Say "as soon as the 14-day test wraps." Approval timing is Google's call.
- Don't use the Android robot or the Google Play badge in "coming soon" art. The badge rules forbid it before the app is live.
