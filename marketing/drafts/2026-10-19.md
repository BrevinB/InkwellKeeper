## Reddit r/Lorcana — collector tip (text post)

*Only if the kit's launch-weekend price post didn't go up this week. Otherwise hold this one for next week.*

**Title:** Three-pile system for sorting a new set after launch weekend (so you don't re-sleeve everything twice)

> Every set release I end up with a pile of pulls on my desk and no plan. This is the system I settled on, in case it helps anyone staring at a Hyperia City stack right now.
>
> **Pile 1: Keepers for the set binder.** One copy of each card number, normal foil or not, depending on whether you're doing a regular or master set. Sort these by collector number *before* sleeving. Hyperia City's main set is 204 cards and the specials are numbered after it (Epics 205–222, Enchanteds 223–240, Iconics 241–242), so leave the specials out of the main pages entirely. A late Enchanted pull then never shifts your layout.
>
> **Pile 2: Playsets / deck candidates.** Copies 2–4 of anything you'd actually play. Keep them in a separate box or trove, not in the binder. Pulling cards out of binder pages for decks is how binders end up with holes nobody remembers.
>
> **Pile 3: Bulk.** Everything else. Rubber band it by ink and write the set on the box. You'll thank yourself at the next trade night.
>
> **Two things I'd do before trading anything:**
> - **Track foils and normals as separate cards.** They're priced separately, and a foil copy of a common can be worth more than the normal of a rare. If your list lumps them together you'll undervalue trades.
> - **Don't price pulls off presale listings.** Several Hyperia City singles are listed 78–84% lower now than their presale prices (e.g. Wasabi - Future Thinker $24.31 → $5.35 in our 7-day data). Week-one prices are still settling. *(Spot-check against TCGplayer before posting; cut this bullet if it doesn't hold up.)*
>
> How's everyone else handling specials: back of the set binder, or a separate binder?
>
> *(I track all this in Ink Well Keeper, a free iOS app I build. It tracks which binder/box every copy is in. Happy to answer questions, but the system works fine with a pen and a notebook.)*
