## Reddit r/Lorcana — collector tip post

**Title:** PSA for anyone chasing Attack of the Vine enchanteds: launch prices are cooling — some patience math

**Body:**
I track daily prices across every Lorcana chase card (483 printings), and the pattern from every recent set is repeating with Attack of the Vine: chase cards spike at launch while supply is thin, then drift down for weeks as more product gets opened.

This week alone: Rapunzel & Flynn Rider - Unlikely Pair went $289 → $232 (-20%), Meilin Lee - Popular Red Panda $124 → $101 (-19%), and most other AOV chase cards drifted down too.

The practical takeaway if you want a specific AOV enchanted: unless you're sitting on trade bait that's also spiking, buying in the first month of a set has historically been the worst window. The same drift happened with Whispers — its chase cards kept sliding for 6+ weeks after launch before flattening.

Nothing here is financial advice and thin markets do weird things (I filtered out several "gainers" this week that were obvious blips, not demand). But if you're deciding between "buy the single now" and "crack packs for a month and buy whatever's left" — the price curve has been on the patient side lately.
