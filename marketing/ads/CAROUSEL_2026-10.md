# Carousel ad — "one feature per slide" (Oct 2026)

A 5-card Instagram/Facebook carousel ad. It follows the standard app-ad layout: headline on one side, a real screen in a phone on the other, alternating sides from slide to slide. It's done in our own look (navy → plum gradient, gold accents) rather than the white/purple template competitors use. Slides are 1080×1350 (4:5). They're in `marketing/drafts/images/2026-10-20/ad-carousel/ad-1..5.png` (gitignored) and are generated by `marketing/ads/src/make_carousel_ad.py`.

**Call to action:** Install Now. **Destination:** App Store (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ink-well-keeper/id6754206379).

## Running it

- **Objective:** App promotion → app installs. This needs the app registered in Meta's App Dashboard with the iOS App Store ID. With no SDK installed, Meta reports installs via SKAdNetwork only, so judge results in App Store Connect (App Analytics → Sources) and RevenueCat rather than trusting Meta's install counts.
- **Audience:** start broad (US, 16+, Advantage+ audience) with "Disney Lorcana" and "trading card games" as suggestions. Lorcana is niche enough that tight interest targeting starves delivery.
- **Budget:** about $10–15/day for 7 days before judging. Kill or iterate on the creative by day 4 if CTR is under ~0.8%.
- **Test against:** the Enchanted price image (`ig-01-enchanted-prices.png`) as a single-image ad. Real price data has been our best organic hook.
- Turn off "Advantage+ creative enhancements" (auto music, auto-cropping, text variations). They tend to wreck carefully composed slides.

## Before it runs

- **Captures.** Slides 2–4 are fresh Oct 20 captures from a simulator seeded with demo data (`xcrun simctl launch <sim> co.brevinb.Inkwell-Keeper -seedDemo YES` on a fresh install, saved in `~/Desktop/InkwellKeeper-Screenshots/ads-2026-10/`). Slide 1 (real camera, July) and slide 5 (import, Nov 2025) are older captures and still show the old tab bar.
- **No App Store badge.** Apple's official badge file couldn't be downloaded from here. Meta's own "Install Now" button does the same job, so slide 5 uses the gold "Free on iOS" pill. If you want the badge anyway, use Apple's unmodified badge artwork from the Apple Marketing Tools site.
