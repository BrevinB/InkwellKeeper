## Monday price post (Oct 19 morning)

Lorcast prices for set 14 are empty until the cards are on sale. Our cron pulls them daily at 06:00 UTC. On Monday morning:

```sh
python3 - <<'E'
import json, urllib.request
API = "https://29kwvipys3.execute-api.us-east-2.amazonaws.com"
cards = json.load(open("Inkwell Keeper/Data/hyperia_city.json"))["cards"]
rows = []
for c in cards:
    try:
        r = json.loads(urllib.request.urlopen(urllib.request.Request(f"{API}/prices/{c['uniqueId']}", headers={"User-Agent": "IWK-marketing/1.0"}), timeout=15).read())
        rows.append((c["name"], c["rarity"], r))
    except Exception:
        pass
print(len(rows), "priced"); [print(x) for x in rows[:60]]
E
```

Only Enchanted and Epic prices exist if the data update has added those cards. Otherwise, take them from Lorcast directly (`/v0/sets/14/cards`, `prices.usd_foil`). Quarantine anything with fewer than 3 days of history or with a single sale. Week-one spikes are noise, so say "launch-weekend" rather than "worth".
