# Ink Well Keeper 3.1.0 — App Store Release Notes

## App Store Connect "What's New" text (4000 char limit)

```
The import update! 3.1 makes it easier than ever to bring your collection into Ink Well Keeper — plus price history, better prices, and powerful set filters.

IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE
• Official Disney Lorcana app: paste your collection backup link and your whole collection imports directly — no other app can do this
• Collectr: import your CSV export
• Dreamborn: imports are now dramatically faster (seconds, not minutes) and correctly handle Enchanted, Epic, and Iconic printings
• LorcanaExporter conversions supported in all formats

SMARTER PRICES
• Price history charts on every card — watch values trend over time
• See both Market price and TCGplayer Low for every card
• Foil cards now show true foil market prices

FIND ANYTHING IN A SET
• Filter any set by ink color, rarity, variant, ownership, and price range
• Sort by card number, price, or rarity

POLISH
• Card text shows real Lorcana symbols (exert, ink, strength, willpower, lore) instead of {E} codes
• Every set's card images now load instantly, even offline
• Attack of the Vine rarities corrected (Epic / Enchanted / Iconic)
• Many scanning and collection fixes

Questions or ideas? Reach out through Settings → Support — the app is built on your feedback.
```
